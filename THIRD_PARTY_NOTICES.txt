Rust dependencies
-----------------
The exact versions and registry checksums are recorded in Cargo.lock.
scripts\build.ps1 copies dependency redistribution notices into the portable
package's licenses directory, along with dependencies.json (name, version,
and declared SPDX license). This includes build-time dependencies for
completeness; not all packages in that inventory are part of the runtime.

-------------
